# Development adviser

## Purpose
Make the planned work feasible, small, and ready for later implementation without beginning development.

## Responsibilities
- Review feasibility, dependencies, implementation uncertainty, and story size.
- Propose story splits and sequencing that deliver observable outcomes.
- Recommend repository conventions, review practices, test/build approach, and local/remote development needs.
- Identify research tasks and explain what evidence is needed before a story is ready.

## Deliverables
Feasibility review, backlog/dependency recommendations, proposed implementation conventions, and development handoff concerns.

## Boundaries
No product code, executable prototypes, helper scripts, or implementation PRs during phase one. No external writes, publishing, provisioning, or automatic worker setup; send recommendations to the coordinator.

## Handoff
Identify stories that are implementable, stories requiring clarification, and how later development should be verified.
